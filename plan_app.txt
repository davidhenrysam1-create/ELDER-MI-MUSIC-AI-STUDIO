1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & PLATFORM OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Elder Mi Music AI Studio is a professional, interactive, AI-powered music production
platform tailored specifically for Afrobeats and related African music genres
(Afro-pop, Afro-fusion, Afro-soul, Amapiano-influenced Afrobeats, South African-
inspired vocal arrangements, Highlife, and Bongo Flava).

The system serves both emerging African bedroom producers and seasoned studio engineers
by providing accessible, intelligent AI workflows alongside granular, industry-standard
digital audio workstation (DAW) controls.

The platform is anchored around three dedicated, non-generic workspaces:
  1. Sam Vocal Studio (Vocal arrangement, choir, harmonies, ad-libs, doubling, transformation)
  2. Sam Mix Studio (Multitrack stem mixing, AI balance assistant, EQ/dynamic channels, spatial)
  3. Sam Mastering Studio (Loudness analysis, tonal balancing, LUFS targeting, release exports)

2. RECOMMENDED PROGRAMMING LANGUAGES & TECHNICAL STACK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To deliver a high-performance, low-latency, modern production environment that runs
smoothly on mobile and tablet touchscreens while handling heavy AI audio synthesis,
a dual-tier decoupled architecture is optimal:

A. CLIENT APPLICATION (Native Android Studio DAW):
   - Language: Kotlin (100% Kotlin 2.2+)
   - UI Toolkit: Jetpack Compose (Material Design 3 with custom glassmorphic DAW canvas)
   - Concurrency & Reactive State: Kotlin Coroutines & StateFlow / SharedFlow
   - Audio Engine:
     * AndroidX Media3 (ExoPlayer) for high-fidelity multi-stream playback and caching
     * Android AudioTrack / Oboe (C++ high-performance audio if ultra-low latency needed)
     * Android Visualizer & Canvas rendering for real-time 60fps waveform & spectrum analysis
   - Networking & Serialization:
     * Retrofit 2 + OkHttp (with 60-second timeouts for audio payloads)
     * Kotlinx.serialization (Type-safe JSON parsing)
   - Local Cache & Storage:
     * Android Scoped Storage / Private Cache for audio buffers, stems, and project files
     * Android Room Database or DataStore for offline project metadata and export history
   - Cloud AI Integration:
     * Google Gemini API (gemini-3.5-flash) for "Improve My Prompt", natural language
       mix/mastering parameter generation, and vocal arrangement orchestration

B. AUDIO DSP & AI MICROSERVICE BACKEND:
   - Language: Python 3.11+
   - API Framework: FastAPI (Asynchronous REST + WebSockets for job progress telemetry)
   - Audio Processing & DSP Libraries:
     * FFmpeg / PyDub (Transcoding, stem extraction, normalization, sample rate conversion)
     * Librosa / SoundFile / Scipy (LUFS loudness, spectral analysis, true peak, dynamic range)
     * Demucs / Spleeter (AI stem separation: vocals, drums, bass, instruments)
     * PyTorch / ONNX Runtime (Vocal synthesis, harmonization, pitch correction)
   - Background Task Queue: Celery + Redis or Google Cloud Tasks
   - Cloud Storage: Google Cloud Storage / Supabase Storage for secure stem and master blobs

WHY THIS COMBINATION IS BEST:
1. Low Latency & High Responsiveness: Kotlin + Jetpack Compose allows hardware-accelerated
   rendering of complex audio timelines, animated meters, and multi-lane waveform views without
   garbage-collection jitter or web-browser audio thread throttling.
2. Heavy Compute Isolation: AI vocal generation and neural stem processing require GPU compute
   that cannot run directly inside a mobile device without draining battery or crashing RAM.
   Offloading heavy synthesis to a Python microservice keeps the Android client fluid and responsive.
3. Clean Separation of Real vs. Simulated Audio: Audio measurements (LUFS, Peak, True Peak,
   Stereo Correlation) are computed mathematically via real DSP algorithms, while creative
   reasoning (prompt interpretation, arrangement logic) is handled by Gemini AI.

4. DETAILED SPECIFICATION OF THE THREE WORKSPACES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. SAM VOCAL STUDIO
- Purpose: AI-assisted vocal arrangement environment specifically tuned for Afrobeats.
- Functional Modules:
  1. AI Chorus Generator: Upload instrumental/lead, request catchy call-and-response chorus.
  2. AI Harmony Generator: High, middle, low third/fifth harmonies, octave doubles, unisons.
  3. AI Ad-Lib Generator: Sung responses, chants, hype vocal stabs, rhythmic fills.
  4. South African-Inspired Choir Generator: Layered ensemble harmonies, sustained refrains,
     group call-and-response with controls for choir size, energy, and density.
  5. AI Vocal Doubler: Natural humanized stereo doubling with subtle pitch and time variances.
  6. Authorized Voice Transformation: Timbre modification with mandatory consent gating.
  7. Interactive Visual Timeline: Multitrack audio waveforms with dedicated lanes for Lead,
     Chorus, Harmony 1/2/3, Choir, Ad-lib, and Doubler. Full Mute/Solo/Pan/Volume per lane.

B. SAM MIX STUDIO
- Purpose: Stem mixing console and AI mix balance assistant for multitrack productions.
- Functional Modules:
  1. Stem Upload & Validation Zone: Drag-and-drop or file picker supporting WAV, MP3, AIFF, FLAC,
     and ZIP stem archives.
  2. FL Studio & Cubase Import Guidance: Step-by-step visual documentation explaining how to
     export consolidated stems from major DAWs to avoid plugin dependency mismatches.
  3. AI Mixing Assistant: Natural language prompt input with "Improve My Prompt" button
     (powered by Gemini 3.5 Flash) that formats amateur descriptions into audio engineering terms.
  4. "Apply Mixing Recommendations": Displays proposed processing steps (e.g. Sidechain kick
     at 60Hz, High-shelf boost +2dB on lead vocal at 12kHz, stereo spread on guitars).
  5. Interactive Mixer Strips: Gain faders, Pan pots, 3-Band Parametric EQ (Low, Mid, High),
     Compressor threshold/ratio, Reverb send, Delay send, Stereo Width, Mute/Solo/Group.
  6. Afrobeats Presets: Modern Afrobeats, Afro-pop, Afro-fusion, Amapiano Groove.

C. SAM MASTERING STUDIO
- Purpose: Release-ready audio mastering workspace with genuine acoustic analysis.
- Functional Modules:
  1. Precision Audio Analysis: Measured Integrated Loudness (LUFS), Short-Term Loudness,
     True Peak (dBFS), Peak Level, Stereo Correlation / Width, and Dynamic Spread.
  2. 7 Selectable Mastering Profiles:
     * Streaming-Ready Master (-14 LUFS, -1.0 dBTP)
     * Loud & Energetic Master (-9 LUFS, -0.3 dBTP)
     * Warm Afrobeats Master (Analog saturation, punchy mid-bass)
     * Punchy Club Master (Sub-bass emphasis, aggressive transient punch)
     * Vocal-Forward Master (Lead presence boost, subtle high-end air)
     * Bass-Focused Master (Amapiano log drum clarity, tight low-end limiter)
     * Smooth & Premium Master (Transparent dynamics, open stereo image)
  3. Mastering Controls: Target LUFS, True-Peak Ceiling, Low-Frequency Weight, Stereo Width,
     Dynamic Preserver, Limiter, Analog Saturation.
  4. Master Comparison: Instant A/B toggle with loudness-matched gain compensation to prevent
     "louder sounds better" psychoacoustic bias. Before/After waveform overlay.
  5. Validated Audio Export: High-resolution WAV (24-bit/48kHz, 16-bit/44.1kHz) and 320kbps MP3.

  * Brand & Studio Titles: Gradient text headers with strong weights
